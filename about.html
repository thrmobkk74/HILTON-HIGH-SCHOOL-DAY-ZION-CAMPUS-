HILTON HIGH SCHOOL MUKONO

About School

Hilton High School Ltd was incorporated on 07th October, 2002 and started operating in January 2003. It is a mixed boarding secondary school with O and A levels, founded on the philosophy of the Seventh-day Adventist Church.

Director: Mr. Wadega Paul. Head Teacher: Mr. Katimbo Gordon. Located on Bukasa Hill, approximately 2–3 km from Mukono town along Katosi Road and about 25 km from Kampala.

Registered with the Ministry of Education and UNEB under PSS/H/65 and U1612. The school started with 230 students in 2003 and had 1,170 students as of Dec 2024.

Facilities include science laboratories, library, computer laboratory, classrooms, dormitories, school truck, van and coaster, brass band and more.

The school was officially commissioned by His Excellency Yoweri Kaguta Museveni on Sunday 18th July 2010.
